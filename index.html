Two weeks in

Prepared for Shir Hadani · 11 September 2026 · 57 meals logged across 12 days, 30 Aug – 10 Sep · bloods drawn 1 Sep

Everything is logged in the moment rather than remembered afterwards, so the portions and the timings are real. Nothing here is tidied up — the skipped lunches, the barbecue and the ice cream are all in it. The patterns below come only from what the log actually records.

What the two weeks show

1. Something sweet in the evening, 7 nights out of 12

Halva, grapes, cookies, ice cream, carrot cake. It is the most consistent thing in the whole log. He confirmed unprompted that it happens most nights and is not new.

This is the question you deferred the snack plan for. There is now an answer.
2. Lunch is the fragile meal, not breakfast

Across the fortnight lunch was skipped once entirely (poker tournament), improvised from whatever was in the house twice, and eaten late more than once. Breakfast and dinner held steady throughout.

Three of those were driven by the day’s shape or an empty fridge, not by appetite.
3. Breakfast protein improved once the friction went

The early misses were avocado toast with no egg. The cause was not reluctance — it was having to cook eggs in the moment. Since batch-boiling, protein has been present in almost every breakfast.

9 of 11 breakfasts carried protein; the misses cluster in the first few days.
4. He shrinks portions rather than avoiding foods

Half a burger at the barbecue. A lettuce wrap instead of a bun at the sports bar. The small ice cream rather than none. This is a repeated, deliberate strategy and it appears to be sustainable.

Worth building on rather than replacing.
5. Eating out went better than expected

Four restaurant meals in the fortnight and a visible choice in each: a salmon bowl over the alternatives, a niçoise salad for breakfast twice, the lettuce-wrapped burger. The only clear compromise was fried rice at Panda Express and the sweet potato fries.

6. Supply decides meals more than intention does

Twice in two weeks the house ran out of something and the meal changed. No cucumbers or tomatoes on 3 Sep, no seeded bread on 6 Sep so naan was used instead. The recommended shop has largely not happened yet.

Likely the cheapest single intervention available.
7. Shabbat is genuinely different, and that is fine

Friday dinner and Saturday lunch are the two carb-heavy meals of the week — challah, rice, potatoes, cake — and He says plainly that he allows himself more. Both meals are in the log as they actually were.

Worth planning around rather than planning against.

Bloods — 1 September

All 59 results came back in range and three clinicians signed them off as normal. The metabolic picture is good: HbA1c 5.4% against a 5.7% prediabetes threshold, fasting glucose 87, and a strong lipid panel — HDL 77, triglycerides 59, cholesterol-to-HDL ratio 2.4. Thyroid is normal at 2.14, which rules out a metabolic factor working against the plan.

The one worth your attention is vitamin D at 30 ng/mL. It is inside the range, so nobody flagged it, but the lab’s own note says values above 30 may be more appropriate for people with risk factors. Every other result sits comfortably mid-range; this one sits on the line — and it is the only one where diet and supplementation are the lever.
